## What is still partial
- Opening, per-invoice items and loans are now READ (T19c): statements, the account and reminder lists, the aging screen and the cash forecast use them. What is still approximate: an opening item has no allocation table, so a payment that no plan item took (the "unallocated credit") is applied to the opening items oldest due date first, then to the part of the opening line without detail; this FIFO rule is in `domain/opening-items.ts` and is the only rule on every screen, but it is a rule and not a recorded allocation. A bounced or disputed payment is not counted, as before. Avoirs are not applied to opening items. The aging with a plant filter drops the opening position (it has no plant).
- Loan and leasing instalments carry no "paid" mark: the forecast shows instalments due from today only, and the instalments are shown to owner and app_admin only (lender and reference are not for the office roles). They are not yet in the J-1 due list.
- Charges approval above the upper limit (`decide_charge`) does not carry the step-up mark in the database (SECURITY-DEBT, T19c A); only outgoing payments moved behind it.
- HT/TTC is stored and shown, the ledger stays HT.
- Charges do not feed `finance_entry` and the document is a reference, not a file; the export is CSV, not Excel. Charges must feed `finance_entry` from 1 Dec: that is T19b with the monthly owner table (decided, NEXT-STEPS).
- Reminders and charges screens have e2e coverage for the main path only; there is no PDF of the reminder log. The forecast and the aging have no PDF and no Excel (CSV for the aging only).
- The "stale second factor" dialog on the request page is proved at the door and in the service (integration) but not by a browser test: the e2e user signs in fresh, so the factor is always present there.
- Items out of scope as stated: historical posting path, cash count with variance, bank reconciliation, month close, checklist, monthly table, SMS/GPS, payroll, portals.
