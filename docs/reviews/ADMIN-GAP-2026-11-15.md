## 0. The structural finding that colours everything

**KAYAS office roles are money-blind by design (D81, D126–D139).** `office_sales` and `office_purchases` *write* orders, payments and supplier invoices, then **never read them back** (no SELECT policy, no RETURNING). Money is read by `owner`, `app_admin`, `support` only (`MONEY_READ` in `permissions.ts`). The new organisation assumes the opposite: the Responsable owns `Soldes_Clients`, `Echeancier`, relances (P4) and the client statements; the Assistante owns supplier balances, cash count, bank reconciliation and charges (Structure des classeurs, "Visibilité" column). Today an office user cannot see the cheque they just entered. Changing this touches D81, D126–D130, D132, D139 and gate G2, and needs a numbered decision and the RLS visibility-matrix tests (`visibility-matrix.test.ts`). This is a decision for Ali (Question 1) and it gates A–D below.

The second structural finding: **the live doors stamp "today".** An order refuses `deliveryDate < today` (`order-service.ts:78`, `DELIVERY_BEFORE_ORDER`), a BL's `load_date` and an instrument's first event are the request-clock date (`delivery-service.ts:219`, `instrument-service.ts:127`; the guard says "the first event is dated the day of receipt", 0037). There is no backdated posting path. The only way to a past date today is the simulation clock, which is refused on a real-data database. So Oct/Nov (and Jan–Sep) cannot be loaded "through the existing doors" as dated sales, receipts, purchases and payments. The history layer (0215) takes dated rows but is **quantity-only for deliveries** and amount-only for expenses and creates no invoice, no ledger movement and no payment. See F16.

## A. Sales and delivery lines

### A1 — BL entry/import with discharge method, class, volume, site, client, centrale — **PARTIAL · L · MUST**
- **EXISTS:** a BL is a truck load of a `sales_order`, created by the plant (`deliveries.write` = owner, app_admin, partner, plant, plant_manager; `delivery-service.ts`, 0041, 0043). Discharge method `GOULOTTE`/`POMPE` on the order and on the BL (0232, required with no default since 0235; `create_order_with_method`); class = `concrete_type`; site = `client_site` (0019); centrale = order's plant (0078); volume in litres. BL printed (`delivery_note`, 0043).
- **MISSING:** an **office** entry or import of paper BLs. The new organisation has the Responsable key BLs from paper into `Ventes` (Structure, "Ventes"); the Correspondance file says "le BL est créé par la centrale dans KAYAS", which is phase 2 for the driver channel and not what the office does on 1 Dec. Paper BL numbers cannot be kept: `delivery.bl_no` must match `^BL-[0-9]{4}-[0-9]{5,}$` and is generated by the sequence (`nextDocNumber`, 0031). A BL also requires a truck and a recette (NOT NULL) that a paper line does not give.
- **Looked in:** `delivery-service.ts`, `0041`, `0043`, `0232`, `0235`, `importers.ts` (11 kinds, none is a sale or BL), `src/app/[locale]/(protected)/` (no office BL entry screen; `loads` is a list).
- **Role:** plant roles and owner/app_admin create BLs; office roles read none (`deliveries.read` is ALL, but money columns are hidden).
- **Gap to close:** a flagged "historical import / office-entered paper BL" door (explicit business date, paper number kept as `bl_ref`, source/batch columns) — see F16.

### A2 — Price per m³, grid vs agreed, HT/TTC basis, VAT — **PARTIAL · M · MUST/SHOULD**
- **EXISTS:** `sales_order.unit_price_ht_centimes`, `vat_rate_bp` copied from parameter `vat_rate_bp` at creation, totals HT/VAT/TTC with a CHECK (0032). Official grid per class and method: `price_grid` (0232), shown as a **reference** on the order form and detail, never written to the order price (T17 G8, `grid.read` = owner, app_admin, office_sales). Agreed or special price: `client_price_override` (0026), `price_override` with owner approval (0047, 0083, `orders.price_override` = owner/app_admin); provisional price status (`order_price_status`, 0207).
- **MISSING:** a **HT/TTC basis per client** in the live ledger. The only `tax_basis` is in the history layer (`history.delivery.tax_basis`, 0215). Orders are always HT with a single VAT rate. Looked in: `0018_client.sql`, all migrations (`grep tax_basis|price_basis`).
- **Role:** price reading and writing is money (owner/app_admin); office_sales sees the grid reference only.

### A3 — BL uniqueness, duplicates, gaps, cancelled BL — **PARTIAL · S · SHOULD**
- **EXISTS:** `bl_no UNIQUE` (0041); gapless generation inside the creating transaction (`next_doc_number`, rolled-back transaction returns the number, 0031); cancelled BL = reversing row (`delivery.reverses_id`, exact negation, mandatory reason ≥1 char, 0041) with a cancellation workflow (`/cancellations`, 0090); history import reports duplicate BL row numbers and a gap **count**, never rejecting or fixing (IMPORT-EXCEL §7.4).
- **MISSING:** uniqueness **per site** is not a concept (the number is global, unique per year sequence). **Gap detection on the live series** (the checklist line "aucun trou dans la série des numéros de BL") has no screen: `day_close_status` (0107) lists fuel variance and stock items, not BL gaps; `/anomalies` lists risk reports, not numbering. For paper numbers imported later the duplicate and gap detection exists only in the history importer.

### A4 — Client PO, invoice from BLs, invoice numbering, periodic invoicing — **PARTIAL · M · MUST (PO) / LATER (cycle)**
- **EXISTS:** invoice from delivered BLs (`create_invoice_from_deliveries`, 0067), "delivered, not invoiced" queue (`/invoices/queue`, 0066), invoice numbers `FA-YYYY-nnnnn` from the same gapless sequence (0031), a cancelled invoice is an avoir `AV-` (0069), deadline "invoice by end of delivery month" (law 69-21, `deadlines-service.ts`), invoice sequence loader for the last 2026 number (importer kind `invoice_sequence`).

A **billing cycle per client** (fortnightly/monthly): no such field; invoices are cut manually from the queue.

### A5 — Client name normalisation, client code, salesperson — **PARTIAL · M · SHOULD**

### F16 — Import tooling for the new workbooks — **PARTIAL · L · MUST**
**What exists (exactly):**
- **Master-data importer (T2, `pnpm import:excel`)**: eleven kinds only — `concrete_types, components, recipes, clients, suppliers, trucks, pumps, selling_prices, opening_balances, cheques_in_transit, invoice_sequence` (`importers.ts`, `workbook-import.ts`). Dry run inside a transaction always rolled back; configurable column mapping per sheet (`--init-mapping`); date format declared not guessed; amounts to integer centimes; refuses 1904 dates, formulas (unless allowed), ambiguous numbers; all-or-nothing one transaction; file-hash idempotency (same bytes refused); serialised by advisory lock; typed phrase `IMPORT <sha256 of file+mappings+opening date>` plus a fresh TOTP step bound to the operation; rollback archives and cancels, never deletes; rows go through the same services as the screens so actor, RLS and audit apply; report has counts and row numbers only.
- **History importer (T11, `pnpm import:history`)**: monthly delivery and expense files of one plant and kind; dry-run, stage, decisions screen (`/history/staging`), per plant and month **totals comparison** (rows read vs imported vs quarantined, SUM m³ or MAD), apply with `HISTORY <set hash>` + TOTP, rollback marks the batch. Rows flagged `source = 'history_import'` + `batch_id`. 
**What does not exist:**
- No importer for **sales lines with price and invoice**, **receipts (payments) with allocation**, **purchases with supplier invoices**, **supplier payments**, **charges** as ledger rows. The history layer carries deliveries as **quantity only, "never a price"** and expenses as amounts for reports; it "never creates an invoice or a ledger movement" and is not shown to office roles (`history.money` = owner, app_admin, accountant).
- No **per-batch totals control for the master importer** beyond row counts (no sum of m³ or amounts shown before commit); the history importer has it.
- No backdated ledger path (see §0), so the requirement "insert through the existing doors with actor and audit chain" **cannot be met for Oct/Nov dated rows** without a new door. The existing-door route only works for master data and opening balances.
- Marker "historical import" + source file exists on the history layer (`source`, `batch_id`); nothing equivalent on `sales_order`, `payment_instrument`, `purchase`.
- Mapping is by named sheet and header, so the new bridge workbooks (`Ventes`, `Encaissements`, `Achats`, `Paiements`, `Charges`) need new kinds and new mapping files; the T11 mapping files describe the **old** monthly files.
- The importer runs from the server shell, owner/app_admin only; there is no office-facing upload screen (acceptable for a one-off).

### F17 — Known source defects — **PARTIAL · M · MUST**
| Defect | Behaviour today |
