# KAYAS — brand guide (adopted 8 Nov 2026)

> **Formerly BELGOBETON.** The working name BELGOBETON is obsolete (decision 204). This file is the brand spec distilled from the guide, plus the mapping from the old name to the new one.

## Status of the assets (read first)

- The logo files in `public/brand/svg/` are the SVGs of the FLUX trace package (`docs/brand/KAYAS-FLUX-TRACE-README.md`). **They are a TRACE of a raster, not an original vector master.** The drawing was generated by an image model in an earlier chat; **nothing is cleared**: no name or trademark search (OMPIC), no similarity check, no ownership established. Do not print, mark vehicles, embroider or register before that is done.
- Never edit, recolour, redraw, outline, stretch or add effects to the logo; do not hand-write an imitation.
- Icons in `public/icons/` are used exactly as delivered (not regenerated). Photos of the guide are AI-generated and are not used anywhere.

## Typography (deliberate substitution)

The guide names Helvetica Neue and Geeza Pro (Apple system fonts, not redistributable). The app instead bundles, self-hosted from `public/fonts/` with their SIL OFL licence texts beside them (`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`): **Inter** (Latin, numbers, units, including m³ which Noto lacks) and **Noto Naskh Arabic** (Arabic). Visual substitutes, not metric-identical; chosen for the same look on Android and Apple phones. SHA-256 of both files is checked by `src/ui/fonts.test.ts`. Numbers, phone numbers and units stay in `dir="ltr"` spans inside RTL screens.

# Brand spec (source: KAYAS Brand Guidelines 01, study FLUX v0.2)

Status of the source: the PDF is titled "Proposition d'identité, pour revue et choix". The owner of the project has decided to ADOPT the name KAYAS
and this identity. Everything below is the extracted, usable part. Do not invent anything that is not here.

## Name
- Brand name: KAYAS (always upper case in the logo; in running text "KAYAS").
- The former working name BELGOBETON is OBSOLETE and must disappear from everything the user, the owner, a driver, a client or a supplier can see.
- Descriptor (separate from the logo, never fused to it, never part of the drawing): "Béton prêt à l'emploi". In Arabic UI use a neutral phrase, not a slogan.
- Forbidden: adding a slogan to the logo drawing (the guide's own example "LA FORCE SANS LIMITES" is a counter-example, never use it).

## Logo files (superseded: see "Status of the assets" above; the SVG trace package replaces the old rasters)
- kayas-symbol-color.png      353x279   the K symbol alone (forest + lemon accent)
- kayas-signature-color.png   1278x279  symbol + name, horizontal, PRIORITY version
- kayas-wordmark-only.png     891x209   the name alone
- kayas-signature-black.png   1278x279  one ink, black (same silhouette)
- kayas-signature-white.png   1278x279  white reserve, for dark flat backgrounds
Caveat: these are low-resolution rasters with a faint light fringe on the edges. Good for screens up to about 2x of 320 px wide. NOT for print, vehicles, embroidery.
The guide itself says a real vector master must be built after the design is validated. Do NOT redraw or "improve" the logo, do NOT hand-write an SVG imitation, do NOT stretch,
recolor, outline or add effects. If a crisp SVG is needed, mark it as an open item for the designer; an automatic trace may only be an interim, labelled as such and never presented as the master.
Rules: clear space x = 1/4 of the visible symbol height on all four sides (more on photos). Signature minimum 120 px wide on screen, below 160 px prefer the symbol alone with the name carried
by the context. Symbol alone sizes tested: 100 / 48 / 24 px. Backgrounds: light = colour version, dark forest = white version, lemon = black version (after test), photo = white cartouche.
Forbidden: stretching, changing the symbol/name ratio, closing the openings of the symbol, adding a slogan.

## Colours (sRGB), roles and proportions 60 neutral / 30 structure / 10 accent
- Forêt   #01592B  RGB 1/89/43     structure, the name, primary buttons, headings
- Citron  #9ADA02  RGB 154/218/2   accent, markers, small highlights (never for body text)
- Graphite #292929 RGB 41/41/41    text complement
- Craie   #F5F5F5  RGB 245/245/245 backgrounds, breathing space
Measured contrast (guide): Forêt on white 8.52:1 ok; white on Forêt 8.52:1 ok; Forêt on Citron 5.03:1 ok; white on Citron 1.69:1 AVOID for text.
Working thresholds: 4.5:1 body text, 3:1 large text; for buttons choose the pair that passes 4.5:1. Never use colour alone for an error or a confirmation.
Error red (guide, for form errors only): a red on a very light red field; the guide does not fix the hex. Keep the app's existing accessible red/amber/green status colours for
STATUS meaning (late, critical, ok) and do not use Citron as a status colour on white.
Dark mode is not defined by the guide: derive it from the same tokens and verify contrast; do not invent new brand colours.

## Typography in the guide (see the substitution above)
- Latin: Helvetica Neue (Regular + Bold). Arabic: Geeza Pro (Regular + Bold). Both are Apple system fonts, NOT redistributable; embedding them in a web app/PWA needs rights.
  Decision needed from the owner: use the platform stack (Helvetica Neue / system-ui first, so Apple devices render the brand font, others fall back) and/or ship a free open substitute
  (an OFL sans for Latin, an OFL naskh-style for Arabic) with the licence file. Do not ship Apple font files.
- Scale in the guide: H1 Bold 40, H2 Bold 24, body Regular 17 (print/slides), caption Regular 12. For the app: body 16 px, line height 1.5.
- Arabic body 22-25 pt in the guide's documents, right aligned, no artificial letter spacing, avoid mixing a phone number and an Arabic sentence on the same line.
- Digits: Latin 0123456789 (the guide also shows Arabic-Indic digits; keep the app's current numeral policy).
- Units seen in the guide: m3 and DH.

## Layout and components (for UI)
- Base 8 px, page margin 48, gutter 24; 12 columns large, 4 on mobile. Corner radius 6 px. Touch target 44 px minimum. Body 16 px, line height 1.5.
- Icons: 24x24 grid, 1.6 stroke, rounded ends, always with a label when ambiguous. The guide's set: location pin, calendar, cube (volume), phone (contact).
- One dominant gesture per page; accent limited to about 10 percent of the surface; text stays in a calm area.
- Primary action: forest fill, white text. Secondary: light fill, forest text. Form fields keep a visible label, states: focus, error, sending, confirmed.
- Accompanying motif: a wide lemon curve (never a logo, never inside the protection area).

## Documents (invoice / delivery note / statements)
- Header: signature colour logo top left, a thin lemon rule under it, document title in forest bold, table header band in forest with white text.
- Footer: legal line "[RAISON SOCIALE] - [ICE / RC] - [ADRESSE]". ALL contact data, ICE, RC, address, phone, e-mail are NOT known yet: the guide marks them "[CONTACT A CONFIRMER]".
  Keep them as configuration the owner fills in; never invent them.
- The quote layout in the guide is a visual example only: amounts, taxes and conditions come from the management system (integer centimes, deterministic code).

## Photos
All photographs in the guide (trucks, plant, workers) are AI-GENERATED illustrations, not real KAYAS fleet or staff. Never use them as real photos in the product, in training material
presented as real, or in any official document. No real-people photos without authorisation.

## Open items (not decided by the guide)
- Trademark / name availability check (OMPIC) and confirmation that the symbol does not copy an existing mark: to be done by the owner before any printing, vehicle marking or registration.
- Real vector master of the logo; legal entity name (raison sociale); contact details; font licensing decision.
